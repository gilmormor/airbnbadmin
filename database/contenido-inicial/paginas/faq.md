FAQ's

Where do I get the keys to the property?

Keys are delivered personally by our concierge upon arrival.

Is the property located in a safe place?

The property is located within the Bonita Village Complex with 24 hour security

How far from the beach is the property?

Playa Las Ballenas is about 7 minutes from the property on foot, crossing the garden of the complex where the common pool is located.

Is the Jacuzzi heated?

All Jacuzzis have a heater

What cleaning and disinfection measures are you implementing?

Our cleaning team does a rigorous daily cleaning of all units and we follow the strictest disinfection controls in all areas of the establishment.

Are there any additional charges for electricity?

All rentals include electricity and air conditioning in all rooms only in the case of Penthouses there is an additional charge for the use of central air for the living and dining areas of $ 20 dollars per night.

What happens to my reservation if my flight is canceled or everything is closed again because of COVID

If due to some force majeure reason you cannot make your trip, you have the option of moving the dates to the future within 45 days of your original reservation.

What are the hours of the complex's common pool?

The pool hours are from 7:00 am to 7:00 pm

Do you allow pets?

This has some flexibility for small, trained pets with a security deposit in case of damage.

FAQ's

Is the Duplex Pool heated?

Pools are not heated only the Jacuzzi.

Am I allowed to play loud music and until what time?

Out of respect for other guests, the rule is that you can listen to music at decent levels and after 11:00 PM it should be at a level you can have a normal conversation

Will we be alone in the apartment or is it shared? 

Each apartment is independent from the others with a private entrance. Shared spaces such as stairs and parking lots green areas are wide so they allow distance.

Is there a restaurant within the complex?

There are no restaurants within the Bonita Village complex. All are located along Playa Las Ballenas.

Does it include breakfast?

We provide complimentary Chef Service for Breakfast
(guests provide the groceries) * only at the Ribera Mar properties

I see the 4 wheels in some photos of the villas, are they included in the rent?

Golf carts and ATV’s are available for rent.

Do you have beach umbrellas available?

We do not have beach umbrellas. Villa Playa and Villa blu have Chaise Lounge available

At what distance from the property are the shops Supermarkets, restaurants

All shops, supermarkets, banks, pastries are about 10-12 minutes from the properties.

Do you have a washing machine and tumble dryer?

All units have a washing machine and clothesline.

What is the cost of the Chef's service?

 
The chef service is complementary for breakfast at Ribera Mar and has a cost of $ 35 dollars for the preparation of lunch or dinner for the group.
