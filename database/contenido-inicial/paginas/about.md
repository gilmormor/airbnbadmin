About Us

What is Ribera Mar

Ribera Mar is a 5 apartment complex located within the Bonita Village Residence, Playa Las Ballenas, Las Terrenas, Samana in the Dominican Republic, built by Constructora Arpel. The Complex consists of 2 Duplex apartments, 2 Penthouse apartments and a ground floor condo. Each detailed in the most exquisite taste, luxury and comfort, all for your personal enjoyment and care.

Distinction

This modern full-of-comfort ocean view Caribbean property at the paradise location of Las Terrenas, Dominican Republic, located in a private 24 hour surveillance residential area at the Bonita Village Residence, just 7 minutes away from Las Ballenas Beach, one of the finest beaches in the Caribbean, and only five minutes away from the most famous fine-dining restaurants, bars and dance clubs at the Fisherman’s Village (Pueblo de Los Pescadores); offering endless moments of fun, relaxation, and loving memories you’ll always remember.

Our philosophy
This exclusive property was designed with the intention of preserving the essence of its surroundings by conserving the green areas and the Salt River running water into the sea, tucked away in a secluded cove by Las Ballenas Beach, offering our guests a unique and private outdoor experience. 

Style and comfort
If a great first impression is what you are expecting, prepare to be amazed more than once. You will be dazzled from the moment you arrive at our property until you settle in. In spite of the proximity to the life and movement of the Fisherman’s Village, you will enjoy quiet moments and privacy due to its privileged location at the end of Las Ballenas Beach
